# Fase 1 — Beginner: 1.4 To Be (am/is/are)

Modul ini membahas kata kerja "to be" dalam bentuk present tense: am, is, are. To be adalah salah satu elemen paling sering dipakai dalam bahasa Inggris, digunakan untuk menyatakan identitas, sifat, kondisi, dan lokasi.

## 1. Pendahuluan

### 1.1 Konteks

Modul ini melanjutkan 1.3 Personal Pronouns secara langsung. Bentuk to be yang dipakai (am, is, atau are) ditentukan sepenuhnya oleh subject pronoun di depannya.

### 1.2 Tujuan Pembelajaran

Setelah modul ini, pembelajar mampu:

1. Memasangkan subject pronoun dengan bentuk to be yang tepat.
2. Membentuk kalimat positif, negatif, dan tanya dengan to be.
3. Menggunakan to be untuk menyatakan identitas, sifat, kondisi, dan lokasi.
4. Menggunakan contracted form (I'm, you're, dst) dengan benar.
5. Menghindari kesalahan umum penghilangan atau penggandaan to be.

## 2. Bentuk Dasar To Be

| Subject | To Be | Contracted Form |
|---------|-------|--------------------|
| I | am | I'm |
| You | are | You're |
| He | is | He's |
| She | is | She's |
| It | is | It's |
| We | are | We're |
| They | are | They're |

Pola dasar: **Subject + to be + complement**

Contoh kalimat:

1. I am a student.
2. You are very kind.
3. He is a doctor.
4. She is from Bandung.
5. It is a small cat.
6. We are ready.
7. They are teachers.

## 3. Fungsi To Be

### 3.1 Menyatakan Identitas

1. I am Rina.
2. He is my brother.
3. This is my father.
4. They are my classmates.
5. We are engineers.

### 3.2 Menyatakan Sifat atau Kondisi

1. She is very smart.
2. The food is delicious.
3. I am tired today.
4. The weather is hot.
5. They are happy about the news.

### 3.3 Menyatakan Lokasi

1. I am at home.
2. The keys are on the table.
3. She is in the kitchen.
4. We are at school.
5. The book is on the shelf.

### 3.4 Menyatakan Usia (dengan angka)

1. I am twenty years old.
2. She is thirty-five.
3. My brother is ten years old.

## 4. Kalimat Negatif

Pola: **Subject + to be + not + complement**

| Positif | Negatif | Contracted |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| I am | I am not | I'm not |
| You are | You are not | You aren't / You're not |
| He is | He is not | He isn't / He's not |
| She is | She is not | She isn't / She's not |
| It is | It is not | It isn't / It's not |
| We are | We are not | We aren't / We're not |
| They are | They are not | They aren't / They're not |

Contoh kalimat:

1. I am not ready yet.
2. You are not late.
3. He is not my teacher.
4. She isn't at home right now.
5. It is not expensive.
6. We aren't from Jakarta.
7. They are not busy today.

## 5. Kalimat Tanya (Yes/No Questions)

Pola: **To be + Subject + complement?**

| Pernyataan | Kalimat Tanya |
|------------|-------------------|
| I am ready. | Am I ready? |
| You are happy. | Are you happy? |
| He is at work. | Is he at work? |
| She is your sister. | Is she your sister? |
| It is your bag. | Is it your bag? |
| We are late. | Are we late? |
| They are students. | Are they students? |

Cara menjawab singkat:

1. Are you a student? — Yes, I am. / No, I am not.
2. Is he your friend? — Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
3. Are they ready? — Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
4. Is it expensive? — Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
5. Are we late? — Yes, we are. / No, we aren't.

## 6. Kalimat Tanya dengan Wh-Question

Pola: **Wh-word + to be + subject?**

1. What is your name?
2. Where are you from?
3. Who is that man?
4. How old are you?
5. Why is she angry?
6. When is your birthday?

## 7. Perbandingan dengan Bahasa Indonesia

| Aspek | Bahasa Indonesia | Bahasa Inggris |
|-------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Kata kerja penghubung | Sering tidak diperlukan ("Saya guru") | Wajib menggunakan to be ("I am a teacher") |
| Perubahan bentuk | Tidak berubah berdasarkan subjek | Berubah: am, is, are |
| Kalimat negatif | Tambah "bukan" atau "tidak" | Tambah "not" setelah to be |
| Kalimat tanya | Perubahan intonasi atau tambah "apakah" | To be dipindah ke depan subjek |

Perbedaan paling mendasar: dalam bahasa Indonesia, kalimat seperti "Saya guru" tidak memerlukan kata kerja penghubung. Dalam bahasa Inggris, kalimat setara wajib menggunakan to be: "I am a teacher", bukan "I teacher".

## 8. Kesalahan Umum

| Kesalahan | Contoh Salah | Koreksi |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| Menghilangkan to be | "I student." | "I am a student." |
| Salah pasangan subject-to be | "She are my friend." | "She is my friend." |
| Menggandakan to be dengan verb lain | "I am go to school." | "I go to school." (tanpa to be, karena "go" sudah verb) |
| Lupa "not" pada kalimat negatif | "He isn't not my brother." | "He isn't my brother." atau "He is not my brother." |
| Salah urutan pada kalimat tanya | "You are ready?" (secara tulisan formal kurang tepat) | "Are you ready?" |

## 9. Ringkasan Modul

To be memiliki tiga bentuk dalam present tense: am (untuk I), is (untuk he/she/it), dan are (untuk you/we/they). To be berfungsi menyatakan identitas, sifat, kondisi, lokasi, dan usia. Pola kalimat negatif dibentuk dengan menambahkan "not" setelah to be, sedangkan kalimat tanya dibentuk dengan memindahkan to be ke depan subjek. Kesalahan paling umum pembelajar Indonesia adalah menghilangkan to be sepenuhnya, karena struktur ini tidak wajib dalam bahasa Indonesia.

## 10. Catatan Lanjutan

Topik berikutnya adalah 1.5 Vocabulary Dasar: warna, benda, anggota keluarga, dan pekerjaan, yang akan langsung memanfaatkan pola to be dari modul ini untuk membentuk kalimat deskriptif sederhana, misalnya "This is red" atau "She is a nurse".
